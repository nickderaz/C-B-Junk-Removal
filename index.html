About us

The two of us built this business on hard work, trust, and showing up on time.

Meet the crew

C&B Junk Removal was founded by Cole Carter and Jack Burich, two UNCW students who grew up in hard-working families and built their business the same way — one job done right at a time.

For the past two years, we've helped local homeowners and businesses with junk removal, moving, pressure washing, exterior cleaning, and general property servicing.

We're not a big corporate outfit — we're your neighbors, invested in this community and committed to treating every property like it's our own.

From a single item pickup to a full property clean-out, you can count on us to show up, work hard, and get it done right.

Why customers call us back

Reliability is where it starts. When you call C & B Junk Removal, you get a fast quote, clear communication, and a crew that shows up on time — every time.

Once we're on site, your property is treated with care. We protect your yard, handle your belongings carefully, and leave the space clean when the job is finished. Nothing gets rushed, and nothing gets left behind.

We also take pride in the finished product. Clean edges, tidy work areas, and results that hold up — the kind of work that speaks for itself.

We built this business to serve Wilmington the way you'd serve a neighbor, not a transaction. That mindset shows in how we treat every customer, and it carries through to our pricing.
